# eSentire

> eSentire is the Authority in Managed Detection and Response (MDR), 
> protecting mid-market and enterprise organizations from cyberattacks 
> 24/7/365. Founded in 2001, eSentire combines AI-driven technology 
> with elite threat hunters to detect and respond to threats before 
> they impact business.

## Core Services
- [Managed Detection and Response (MDR)](https://www.esentire.com/what-we-do/esentire-managed-detection-and-response): 24/7 threat detection, investigation, and response across endpoint, network, log, and cloud.
- [Digital Forensics & Incident Response (DFIR)](https://www.esentire.com/what-we-do/digital-forensics-and-incident-response): Rapid response and investigation for active breaches.
- [Continuous Threat Exposure Management (CTEM)](https://www.esentire.com/what-we-do/preempt-offensive-security): CTEM and advisory programs that identify security gaps and build proactive strategies to address them.

## Technology Platform
- [eSentire Atlas Security Operations Platform](https://www.esentire.com/what-we-do/security-operations-platform): Task-specific Atlas Agents investigate threats at machine speed with full transparency, expert validation, and explainable outcomes you can trust.
